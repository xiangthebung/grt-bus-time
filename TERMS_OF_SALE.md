## What Pro adds

- a countdown for your next departure on the toolbar icon, kept up to date in the background
- arrival alerts 2 to 15 minutes before your bus, if you grant the optional notifications permission
- your closest saved stop first, using a location you have opted into sharing

Saved stops, stop search, live departures and service alerts are on the free plan and stay there. Pro adds features; it does not take any away.

## Availability and accuracy

GRT Next Bus is provided on an as-available basis, and it is worth being blunt about the limits of what it can promise. Departures come from the Region of Waterloo's published GTFS and GTFS-realtime feeds. When those feeds are down, stale, or wrong, the extension is wrong with them, and it labels predictions as live or scheduled so you can tell which you are looking at. Countdowns and alerts also depend on Chrome choosing to run the extension's background timers, which Chrome may delay or skip on battery, under memory pressure, or while the machine is asleep.

**Do not rely on this extension to catch a bus you cannot afford to miss.** It is a convenience, not a guarantee, and no refund is owed because a bus was missed.
